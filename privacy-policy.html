Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 October 2026 · Version 3.0 · Applies to the SelfOS app for iOS and Android and to selfosapp.com

The short version

  • Your life stays on your phone. Tasks, habits, food diary, water, sleep, body measurements, photos and everything you read from Apple Health or Health Connect are stored on your device. We have no copy of it.
  • Health data never leaves your device through us. It is never sent to analytics, never used for advertising, never sold and, starting with version 2.1.0, never stored in iCloud.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking across apps. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not sell or share personal data for advertising.
  • A little technical data does leave the device: optional usage analytics and crash reports (you can turn them off), purchase status, and the words or barcodes you search for in the food database.
  • You are in control. You can export everything, turn analytics off, disconnect health sync and delete all data in the app at any time.

2. How SelfOS is built: local first

SelfOS has no user accounts and no cloud sync. What you enter or import is saved in the app's private storage on your device (the app's web storage, an on-device database, private files and, for widgets, a container shared only between the app and its own widgets). Other apps cannot read it. We cannot see it.

Because the data lives only on your device, deleting the app or losing the phone deletes it too, unless you have exported a copy (see section 9).

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you choose to connect, SelfOS reads the following data from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). SelfOS never writes data to Apple Health or Health Connect.

  • Sleep and sleep stages
  • Workouts
  • Steps
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Blood oxygen (SpO2)
  • Wrist temperature (iPhone only)
  • Distance, active calories burned and total calories burned (Android only)

Data is read only from the day you connect; nothing from before that day is imported. Each sync reads the last 7 days again (never earlier than the day you connected), so a watch that was away from the phone can catch up.

How we use it: only to show your sleep, activity and vitals in the app, to calculate your wellness sleep score and calorie balance, and to keep your bonsai streak. All of this happens on your device.

What we never do with it: we never send it to our servers or to any third party, never include it in analytics or crash reports, never use it for advertising, marketing or data mining, never sell it, never use it to decide on credit, insurance or employment, and never store it in iCloud.

Google Health Connect Limited Use. SelfOS's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control: you choose which data types to allow in the system permission screen. You can disconnect in SelfOS (Profile → Watches and trackers) or withdraw permission at any time in the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect settings (Android). Data already imported stays in SelfOS until you delete it.

9. Your controls

  • Analytics: Profile → Privacy → Usage statistics. Turning it off stops usage analytics immediately and crash reporting from the next app start, and stops subscription events being sent to Google Analytics.
  • Health sync: disconnect in the app or withdraw permission in the Health app or Health Connect.
  • Export: Profile → Data → Export data lets you save a full copy of your data as a file. Please do this before changing or resetting your phone: because your data is kept out of cloud backups, a new phone will not have it otherwise.
  • Delete All Data: Profile → Delete All Data permanently erases everything SelfOS stores on the device, including backups, photos and widget data. It cannot be undone.
  • Data outside your device: it contains no name, email or content. Analytics are deleted after the retention period in section 8 and crash reports after 90 days; the anonymous food-search session holds only a random ID. After Delete All Data, the app starts with a new random identifier, so the old records are no longer connected to you. See our Data Deletion page.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Most of your data is on your device, so you can exercise these rights directly in the app. For data held by us or our providers, email us. We will answer within one month (this can be extended by two months for complex requests, and we will tell you if so). Because we have no accounts and do not attach names or emails to technical data, we usually cannot identify which analytics or server records belong to you. In that case the law does not require us to collect extra information just to identify you (GDPR Art. 11), and these records are handled as described in section 8.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live or work. In Portugal, where the developer lives, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, cnpd.pt); in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

12. Security

Data on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and device encryption when your phone is locked with a passcode. Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We keep data leaving the device to a minimum and do not attach names, emails or content to it. No system is perfectly secure; protect your phone with a passcode.

Lock screen. Reminders can show the name you entered and goal titles on the lock screen, and home-screen widgets show summaries of your day. Task titles and numbers are hidden in iPhone widgets while the phone is locked, but home-screen widgets are visible to anyone who unlocks or holds your phone. You can change notification previews in your phone's settings and choose which widgets to add.

13. Children

SelfOS is intended for people aged 13 and older. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (up to 16 in some EEA countries), please ask a parent or guardian before connecting health data or turning on analytics. For users under 18, the app does not suggest calorie deficits. If we learn that we hold data from a child under 13, we will delete it.
