Delete Your Data in SelfOS

This page explains how to delete the data associated with SelfOS: Self-Care Tracker (developer: Viktoriia Smyrnova), available on Google Play and the App Store.

Delete everything directly in the app

  1. Open SelfOS
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right tab)
  3. Scroll to the Data section
  4. Tap Delete All Data
  5. Type the confirmation word shown on screen and confirm

This immediately and permanently erases everything the app stores on your device. No account or request is needed — the app works without accounts, and your content lives only on your device.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhere it livesOn deletion
Tasks, goals, habits, shopping lists, health entries (water, nutrition, sleep, workouts), body parameters, photos, themes, statistics, local backups Your device only Deleted immediately by the in-app button (or by uninstalling the app)
Anonymous session identifier Our server (Supabase) Signed out by the in-app button. To delete the server row, email us (see below) — processed within 30 days
Anonymous usage analytics & crash reports Google Firebase Raw data is automatically deleted by Google after 2 months; to request earlier deletion, email us
Purchase records Apple / Google / RevenueCat Kept as long as required to honor your purchases and by the stores' tax/accounting rules

Request deletion by email

For anything server-side (the anonymous identifiers above), or if you cannot use the in-app button, email us at selfos.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". No account details are needed — describe your request and we will help within 30 days.
