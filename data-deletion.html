How to delete your SelfOS data

Updated: 14 October 2026 · SelfOS by Viktoriia Smyrnova

SelfOS has no user accounts. Almost everything is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in a few seconds.

1. Delete everything on your device

  1. Open SelfOS and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete All Data and confirm.

This permanently deletes, on this device: your profile and body data, tasks, goals, habits, shopping lists, food diary, water, sleep and activity history, data imported from Apple Health or Health Connect, photos, automatic backups, widget data and scheduled reminders. It cannot be undone. If you might need your data later, export a backup first (Profile → Data → Export data).

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of this from the device. Files you exported and saved elsewhere (for example in Files or a messenger) are not affected; delete them there.

2. Health data

SelfOS only reads from Apple Health and Health Connect and never writes to them, so deleting SelfOS data does not change anything there. To stop SelfOS reading new data, disconnect in Profile → Watches and trackers, or withdraw access in the Health app (iPhone: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → SelfOS) or in Health Connect (Android: Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → SelfOS).

3. Data outside your device

A small amount of technical data is stored by our service providers. It contains no name, email, health values or content you create:

DataHow it is deleted
Usage analytics and crash reports linked to a random app identifierTurn analytics off in Profile → Privacy to stop collection. Collected data is deleted automatically: analytics 14 months after your last use of the app, crash reports after 90 days.
Anonymous food-search session (a random ID only)Contains nothing that identifies you. After Delete All Data or reinstalling, the app uses a new random ID, so the old one is no longer linked to your device.
Purchase recordsKept by RevenueCat, Apple and Google as required by tax and accounting law. Your Apple ID or Google account controls them.
Emails you sent usAsk us by email and we will delete them. Otherwise they are deleted after 2 years.

Because SelfOS has no accounts, we cannot link this technical data to your name or email, so we cannot look it up for you. That is by design: it means there is nothing personal to find.
