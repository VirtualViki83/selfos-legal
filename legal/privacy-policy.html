Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 1, 2025

SelfOS ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data in our mobile application available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

1. Local-First Storage

Your personal data stays on your device.

  • Tasks, goals, habits, and lists stored locally
  • Photos stored only on your device
  • Backup files saved to your device storage
  • No cloud sync or external data storage

2. Data We Process

We process the following data:

  • Subscription data via RevenueCat (purchase validation only)
  • Anonymous analytics (app usage patterns, no personal data)
  • Device type for app optimization (iOS/Android)
  • Crash reports to improve app stability

3. Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export all data anytime via backup feature
  • Delete all data by uninstalling the app
  • Manage subscriptions via App Store or Google Play

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

  • RevenueCat - subscription management
  • Google Play / App Store - payment processing
  • Analytics - anonymous usage statistics

5. Children's Privacy

SelfOS is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us.

In Simple Terms

  • Your tasks, goals, and habits stay on your device
  • We only process subscription and anonymous analytics data
  • No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers
  • You can delete everything by uninstalling the app

Your data, your device, your privacy.
